my school

My school is a big and beautiful place where I go to learn and grow every day. It has
large, clean, and airy classrooms that make studying comfortable, and a huge playground
where my friends and I play sports and games during recess. All of my teachers are
incredibly kind, smart, and always ready to help us understand new concepts. Every morning,
our day begins with a peaceful assembly, which fills us with positive energy.

my hero

My father is my hero and the best person in my life.


He is the one I look up to for all my dreams and goals


He faces all the hard times in life without letting them hurt us


He helps me with my studies and solves all my daily problems.


He is my first teacher who teaches me good manners and ethics


He always guides me to make the right decisions in life


nature

A child’s world feels safest when a father’s hand is held tightly. From early morning school routines to
bedtime stories, a father quietly becomes a guide, a protector and a role model.
Writing a my father essay helps children express love, respect and gratitude for someone who shapes
their values every day. At EuroKids, children are encouraged to talk,
write and reflect on family bonds through simple yet meaningful activities.
