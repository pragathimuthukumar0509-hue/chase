general insurance

General insurance protects your valuable
assets from financial loss caused by unexpected
events. Unlike life insurance, which covers
human life, general insurance focuses on non-life
assets like homes, cars, health, and businesses.

Importance of insurance

Insurance is a vital financial tool that
protects you from unexpected losses and helps
maintain your financial stability

life insurance

Life insurance is a legal contract where an insurance
company promises to pay a cash amount to your
family or chosen beneficiaries when you pass away,
in exchange for regular payments called premiums

risk insurance

In insurance, risk is the chance or uncertainty of
an economic loss caused by an unexpected
and accidental event.
