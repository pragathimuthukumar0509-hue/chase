Importance of Trading

Trading plays an important role in the economy by
helping goods, services, and financial assets
move between buyers and sellers. It also provides opportunities
for businesses and individuals to earn income and grow their investments.

Stock Trading

Stock trading is the buying and selling of shares
of companies in the stock market. Traders buy stocks when they expect their prices to
increase and sell them to earn a profit. Stock trading requires knowledge
of market trends, company performance, and financial conditions.
It can provide good returns, but it also involves the risk of losing money.
